Project EDUCA

Student-Led Nonprofit

Education That Transforms Futures

Project EDUCA works to reduce educational inequality by providing school supplies, educational resources, and learning opportunities for children and youth in underserved communities across Mexico and Brazil.

Project EDUCA serves more than 1,500 students internationally.

Who We Serve
